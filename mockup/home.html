Discover Ouarzazate

Explore the gateway to the desert, where history, culture, architecture and cinema come together.

Why Ouarzazate?

Ouarzazate is known for its traditional architecture, kasbahs, ksours, oasis landscapes, cultural heritage and connection to cinema.

About Ouarzazate

Ouarzazate is a beautiful city in southern Morocco, known for its historic kasbahs, traditional architecture, stunning landscapes and famous film studios. Discover its rich heritage, Amazigh culture and the unique charm of the gateway to the desert.

History

Ouarzazate has a rich history shaped by Amazigh culture and ancient trade routes. Over the centuries, kasbahs and traditional villages developed across the region. In 1928, the modern city began growing as an administrative centre. After Morocco's independence in 1956, Ouarzazate continued to develop, becoming known for tourism and filmmaking.

Geography & Climate

Ouarzazate is located in southeastern Morocco, south of the High Atlas Mountains. It is surrounded by mountains, rocky landscapes, valleys and palm-filled oases. The region has a semi-arid climate, with hot, dry summers, mild winters and limited rainfall.

Culture & Traditions

Ouarzazate is rich in Amazigh heritage and traditional Moroccan culture. Its traditions are reflected in local crafts, traditional music, colorful festivals, regional cuisine and earthen architecture. The region is also known for its hospitality and strong community ties, preserving its cultural identity across generations.

Amazigh Heritage

Ouarzazate has a rich Amazigh heritage reflected in its language, traditions, crafts and architecture. Amazigh communities have preserved their cultural identity through traditional music, colorful carpets, local festivals and historic kasbahs, passing their customs from one generation to the next.

Architecture

Ouarzazate is famous for its traditional earthen architecture, featuring kasbahs, ksour and buildings made from clay and mud bricks.Their distinctive walls, towers and geometric decorations make them an important part of southern Morocco's cultural identity.

Local Life

Local life in Ouarzazate reflects the traditions and hospitality of southern Morocco. Daily activities include visiting traditional markets, practicing local crafts, preparing Moroccan dishes and gathering with family and friends. Amazigh customs and community values remain an important part of everyday life.
